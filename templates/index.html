Required columns: roll_no, name, date, status. Status: PRESENT, ABSENT, HOLIDAY.

How this solves the missing-database problem

  • Students can enter their own verified attendance calendar data while the real database is unavailable.
  • Sundays are automatically treated as holidays.
  • Saturday, local and festival holidays can be marked separately.
  • Once the official CSV is available, it can replace/update these records.
  • The analysis never labels unentered information as official attendance.
